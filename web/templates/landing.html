How it works

  1. Your own copy. The first time you open this site, your browser gets its own database with sample data. Nobody else can see what you add.
  2. Nothing leaves your browser. There is no server: pages, logins and data all run locally (SQLite compiled to WebAssembly, saved in IndexedDB).
  3. Keep your changes. Clearing site data resets everything. Use Admin → Backup & restore to export a copy.
